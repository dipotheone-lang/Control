## 1. What Control processes, and from where

- **Source:** control@ubcsis.com's **own inbox** — mail sent or copied to
  control@ under the §3.1a **Option A (CC discipline)** practice. Staff CC
  control@ on project communications.
- **Not in scope here:** the seven shared functional mailboxes (sales@,
  procure@, …). Reading those is the §3.1a **Option C** expansion, which
  stays gated on this document, O-08 and O-10, and is **not** switched on
  by this draft.
- **Data processed:** sender, recipients, subject, timestamps, thread
  position, and — for non-confidential items — content, to evaluate
  submissions and track obligations.
- **Never processed:** the **body** of a client-confidential item (D-01) —
  tracked as metadata only, never read, never passed to any model or
  service. This draft does not change that.

## 2. Purpose

Obligation and deadline control: ensuring statutory, commercial and
operational deadlines are met, submissions are complete, and the corporate
record is auditable. Control measures **process compliance, not individual
worth** (§12.4), and its outputs are never the sole basis for any
disciplinary or pay decision.

## 3. Lawful basis — TO CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL

Candidate bases under PDPL 151/2020 for processing employees' data for
this purpose:

- **Legitimate operational interest** of the company in meeting its legal
  and contractual deadlines, and/or
- **Performance of the employment relationship** and the company's own
  legal obligations.

Counsel to confirm which basis applies and whether, under the executive
regulations in force since 1-Nov-2025, any registration or filing with the
Personal Data Protection Centre is required for this processing.

## 4. Notification to staff — TO CONFIRM

The CEO states the usage policy is announced to the whole team and that
staff CC control@ on project mail knowingly. To record O-07's notification
element, confirm:

- **Date** the policy/announcement went out.
- **Channel** (email, meeting, signed policy).
- That it stated: what Control reads (control@'s own inbox), the purpose,
  what is recorded, retention (per `retention.yaml`), and the right to
  dispute a finding (§8.4).

Where the announcement did not state all of these, the gap is small and
nameable — a one-line follow-up note closes it, rather than starting over.

## 5. Data subjects and their rights

- **Employees** whose submissions and correspondence reach control@.
- **Counterparties** (clients, suppliers) whose mail is copied to control@.
- Every finding about a person is **contestable via DISPUTE** (§8.4); the
  clock suspends and the CEO adjudicates.

## 6. Retention

Per `config/retention.yaml` (O-10 draft). Mailbox-derived metadata carries
the tightest period (2 years) and does not accumulate until this precondition
and O-08 are on the record.
