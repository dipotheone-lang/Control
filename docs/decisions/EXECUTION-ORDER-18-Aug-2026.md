## 1. THE OPERATING VISION

### ENGLISH

The system runs now. It does not wait for perfect information, because perfect information never arrives and waiting for it is how systems die before they start.

It operates on what is known, records honestly what is not, and closes the difference continuously — from live email traffic, from documents that arrive on their own, and by asking for what does not arrive.

It grows with the volume of information it processes. Every message read makes it know more, and also makes it aware of more that it does not know. Both are progress.

Governance is closed **step by step**, not as a precondition. What can be evidenced today is evidenced today. What needs a qualified human waits for one, visibly, without blocking everything around it.

**The system is useful from month one and more accurate every month after.**

### العربية

النظام يعمل الآن. لا ينتظر اكتمال المعلومات، لأن المعلومات لا تكتمل أبداً، وانتظار اكتمالها هو ما يُميت الأنظمة قبل أن تبدأ.

يعمل بما هو معلوم، ويسجّل بأمانة ما هو مجهول، ويغلق الفارق باستمرار — من حركة البريد اليومية، ومن المستندات التي تصل تلقائياً، وبالطلب لما لا يصل.

ينمو بحجم ما يعالجه من معلومات. كل رسالة يقرأها تزيده معرفة، وتزيده في الوقت نفسه إدراكاً لما لا يعرفه. وكلاهما تقدّم.

وتُغلق الحوكمة **خطوة خطوة** لا كشرط مسبق. ما يمكن إثباته اليوم يُثبت اليوم، وما يحتاج إنساناً مؤهلاً ينتظره ظاهراً دون أن يعطّل ما حوله.

**النظام مفيد من الشهر الأول، وأدقّ في كل شهر بعده.**

**النص العربي هو النص المعتمد.**

## 3. CONFLICTS RESOLVED

### 3.1 D-07 vs. M1 — mailbox scope

D-07 selected Option C: seven shared mailboxes. M1 (under D-52) requires Phase 2 on `control@` only.

**Resolution: M1 governs. D-07 stands as the decided target state, suspended until legal review closes.**

Reason: seven mailboxes multiply the personal-data footprint at the exact moment the lawful basis is unverified and registration is incomplete. Control delivers deadline enforcement, report evaluation and statutory alerting from one mailbox.

**Accepted cost, to be stated in every management report:**
- ETA rejection detection stays **manual** — Control cannot see `accounts@`
- External SLA coverage limited to threads copied to `control@`
- Row 7, the tightest statutory window in the system, is unautomated

**Reactivation of D-07 requires:** legal review complete, or explicit CEO override recorded as a new decision.

### 3.2 D-17 vs. D-13 — special-category data

HSE records are special-category (D-17); the basis is legitimate interests (D-13). Special-category data typically requires a stronger basis.

**Resolution: D-18 makes it moot and becomes a hard rule.** Individual incident reports are metadata-only — never read, so the health data is never processed. **Any future request to read incident content re-opens this and requires CEO decision, not configuration change.** Record the reason in config so it is not relaxed casually.

### 3.3 D-44 vs. usage policy signatures

Email notification is legally sufficient (D-44). The usage policy keeps its eleven signatures anyway (M3).

**Resolution: two documents, two treatments.** PDPL notification by email. Usage policy signed — not for legal sufficiency but because §12.4 warns the system dies if introduced quietly, and a signature is what makes someone read the clause about their right to dispute.
