## Subject

## ════════ ENGLISH ════════

Dear [name],

We are preparing to put an internal compliance system into operation at
United Brothers. It reads the company's `control@ubcsis.com` mailbox,
records which reports and statutory filings arrive and when, and
produces management reporting from that record. It processes personal
data about our own staff, and it will be understood by them as
monitoring whatever we intend by it.

Before it sends a single message to an employee we need four
instruments in place. I am instructing you on all four together because
they interlock, and I would rather they were drafted as one piece of
work than four.

**1. Lawful basis and employee notification — PDPL 151/2020.**
The attached `PDPL-BASIS.md` sets out what the system reads, what it
stores, and for how long, and proposes a basis. Please confirm or
correct the basis, and settle the form of the written notification to
staff.

**2. Retention schedule.**
`RETENTION-SCHEDULE.md` proposes a period per class of record. Please
confirm the statutory minimums that apply — commercial books and
supporting documents in particular — and tell us where our proposal is
below them.

**3. Usage policy.**
`USAGE-POLICY.md` is the document employees will actually read: what
the system measures, what it may inform, what it may never be the sole
basis for, and how to contest a finding. Please review it for anything
that would not survive a labour dispute.

**4. Internal Work Regulations amendment.**
`IWR-AMENDMENT.md` is a draft of the clause defining the system, its
scope, what it records, and permitted use of its outputs. Please draft
the final text, advise on filing, and tell us what form of employee
acknowledgement you want on file.

**Two points are live and we have not assumed our way past them.**

Egypt enacted a new Labour Law in 2025 replacing Law No. 12 of 2003,
with its own executive regulations and transitional arrangements. Every
statement in these drafts about employment procedure is marked
`UNVERIFIED` and needs checking against the current position rather
than pre-2025 practice.

The status of the PDPL executive regulations, and any registration or
data protection officer requirement flowing from them, also needs
confirming.

**On the drafts themselves.** They are starting points, written from
what the system actually does rather than from a template, so that the
slow part of your review is not staring at a blank page. Each one ends
with a section headed *Questions for counsel* — those are the points we
could not resolve and deliberately did not guess at. Anywhere a number
or a period is not established the drafts say `TO BE CONFIRMED` rather
than supplying a plausible figure.

Could you tell us what timetable is realistic? Nothing goes live until
all four are settled, so your answer sets our date rather than the
other way round.

Yours sincerely,

Ahmed Diab
Chief Executive Officer
United Brothers Co. for Contracting, Supplies & Industrial Services

## Notes for Ahmed, not for counsel

**Attach all four, not one.** They interlock: the retention schedule
sets a number the usage policy has to state, and the IWR amendment
references all three. Sending them one at a time turns one review into
four rounds.

**The order of work is in `README.md`** if counsel asks which to start
with: PDPL basis, then retention, then usage policy, then IWR.

**Ask for the timetable explicitly.** Three of these four (O-07, O-08,
O-10) also gate the extended mailbox scope you decided on 16-Aug — so
until they close, Control reads `control@` only, whatever D-07 says.
Their answer sets the Phase 2 date.

**This letter was not sent by Control and cannot be.** §10 closes the
external gate permanently, with no override for seniority or urgency.
Anything leaving the company goes out from a person.
